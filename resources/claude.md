# AV Industry LinkedIn Outreach List — ConferenceCaptioning.com
 
Running log for the daily "AV industry LinkedIn outreach list" scheduled task (previously weekly, now daily with smaller batches so the list doesn't run out of genuinely relevant people too fast). Purpose: build a low-effort drip of genuine LinkedIn connections with AV engineers/technicians, integrators, event production/staging people, broadcast engineers, and AVIXA-adjacent professionals, for relationship-building and organic reach, not cold outreach. Saamer reviews each batch and sends connection requests himself; this task never touches LinkedIn directly.
 
Workflow (updated 2026-09-16): Saamer sends the LinkedIn connection request itself with no note attached. Once someone accepts, he sends a personalized first message, using the "Suggested message" draft below as a starting point in his own voice. Message style: warm and conversational, opens with something specific and genuine about the person (written the way an actual person would say it, not a formulaic "I noticed X."), introduces Saamer as the founder of ConferenceCaptioning in a sentence or two (on-device AI captioning/translation for live events, works without venue internet), asks a genuine question that draws on their specific vantage point, and invites them to share interesting work or grab a short 20-minute call, more like reaching out to build a relationship with someone he admires than a sales pitch. No em dashes anywhere in this doc, by request. Entries drafted before 2026-09-16 used an older "connection-request note" format; those are being rewritten to the new style as encountered below, except for the handful already sent (Paul Konikowski, Urmil Vaidhya, Christina Cruzeiro, Craig Park), whose original drafts are left as historical record rather than rewritten (see the tracker for what was actually sent to them).
 
Referrals (added 2026-09-30): a warm referral from an existing connection is handled outside the dated daily-batch flow above, since these weren't sourced by the daily research task. If Saamer is not yet connected with the referred person and only has an email address, it becomes a warm email introduction referencing the mutual connection; if he's already connected with them on LinkedIn, it becomes a LinkedIn message in the same style as the rest of this doc. Either way it gets its own "Referral via [name]" entry in the From batch column below.
